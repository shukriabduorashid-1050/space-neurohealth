ENVIRONMENT INDEX

Transparent scoring model

The Environmental Risk Index is a prototype weighted score from 0 to 100.

Index = Σ(weight × normalized deviation)

The current demonstration weighting is: CO₂ 30%, radiation 20%, pressure 10%, temperature 15%, humidity 10%, and continuous wakefulness 15%.

Each variable is converted to a bounded 0–100 deviation score before weighting. The score is an analytical interface construct, not a validated medical risk prediction.

CORRELATION

Lagged Pearson correlation

The analysis shifts the environmental series relative to the performance series by 0–4 hourly observations, then calculates Pearson's correlation coefficient.

A positive or negative association does not establish that an environmental factor caused a performance change.

RT
BEHAVIORAL MEASUREMENT

PVT metrics

Reaction time is recorded in milliseconds. Reciprocal reaction time is calculated as 1000 divided by reaction time.

The prototype tracks lapses at 500 ms or above and false starts below 150 ms.

About Space NeuroHealth

◇

A research-oriented exploration platform

Space NeuroHealth is designed as an interactive prototype for exploring how environmental conditions could be analyzed alongside human performance data during spaceflight.

The interface intentionally separates demonstration values from user-provided data and avoids presenting simulated relationships as established physiological facts.
