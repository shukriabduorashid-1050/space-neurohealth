MULTI-PARAMETRIC ENVIRONMENT INDEX

How the Exploratory Score Is Calculated

The Environment Index is a transparent demonstration score rather than a clinical or spacecraft safety rating.

Index = weighted normalized deviations Index = 0.35(CO₂) + 0.20(Radiation) + 0.15(Temperature) + 0.10(Humidity) + 0.10(Pressure) + 0.10(Sleep/Wakefulness)
0–30 Nominal
31–60 Caution
61–100 Elevated Risk

The weights and thresholds are intentionally presented as prototype assumptions. They must be validated against appropriate scientific datasets before being treated as a real risk model.

⚡

Reaction Time

Raw reaction time is retained in milliseconds so the original behavioral measurement remains visible.

1/x

Reciprocal Reaction Time

RRT is calculated as 1000 divided by reaction time in milliseconds. It provides a transformed performance measure that reduces the influence of highly slow responses.

✓

Lapses & Anticipations

Responses above 500 ms are counted as lapses. Responses below 150 ms are treated as anticipatory false starts.

TIME-LAGGED CORRELATION

Why Test Different Time Lags?

Environmental changes and human performance measurements do not necessarily occur at the same moment. The dashboard therefore permits comparison at lags from 0 to 4 hours.

For each selected lag, paired observations are created from the available time series and Pearson's correlation coefficient is calculated.

PROJECT

About Space NeuroHealth

🛰️
RESEARCH CONCEPT

Monitoring the environment around human performance

Space NeuroHealth is an exploratory prototype designed to make environmental and cognitive performance data easier to inspect, compare and communicate.

The concept focuses on the possibility of connecting mission-environment measurements with behavioral performance while keeping the distinction between demonstration modelling and validated science visible to the user.

🌫️

Environment

CO₂, radiation, pressure, temperature, humidity and sleep-related variables.

🧠

Performance

Reaction time and psychomotor vigilance measurements.

📊

Analytics

Descriptive statistics, trends and time-lagged correlation.

🔒

Local Processing

CSV processing occurs locally in the browser without requiring a dataset service.
