PROJECT INFORMATION

About Space NeuroHealth

🧠

Research Prototype

Space NeuroHealth is an interactive research prototype designed to explore how environmental measurements could be connected with human cognitive performance during spaceflight.

The interface combines environmental visualization, interactive simulation, reaction-time testing, dataset ingestion, and exploratory statistics.

🌌

Space Environment

Space missions expose crews to environmental conditions that require continuous monitoring.

Prototype focus: environmental time-series visualization.

🧠

Human Performance

Cognitive and psychomotor performance can be studied using measurable behavioral tasks.

Prototype focus: reaction-time measurements.

📊

Data Analysis

Time-series datasets can be transformed into statistics and visualizations.

Prototype focus: transparent exploratory analysis.

RESEARCH QUESTION

What could an integrated monitoring system reveal?

The prototype explores whether environmental measurements and human-performance observations can be represented together in a way that helps researchers identify patterns, unusual observations, and questions for further investigation.

🛰️

Space Data

Designed to accept time-series environmental data.

📉

Visualization

Interactive charts make changes easier to inspect.

⚡

Reaction Time

Includes a browser-based PVT demonstration.

🔬

Scientific Caution

Demonstration results are separated from verified scientific findings.

NEXT DEVELOPMENT

From prototype to validated research tool
