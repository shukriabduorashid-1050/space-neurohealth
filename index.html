DATA & RESEARCH NOTE

Where the information comes from

This prototype is designed around publicly available space-environment and human-performance research concepts. The values displayed in the interactive simulation are demonstration values and should not be interpreted as direct measurements from an astronaut or as a medical assessment.

ANALYTICAL LAYER

Understanding the Relationship

📈

Environmental Signal

The simulation increases CO₂ across three environmental scenarios. This allows the user to visually inspect how the environmental variable changes.

⚙️

Performance Signal

Reaction-time values change across the same scenarios. A higher reaction-time value represents slower responding within this simplified demonstration.

🔎

Scientific Caution

The visualization can show an association within the demonstration values, but it cannot establish that CO₂ alone caused a change in cognitive performance.

SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS

What this prototype does not claim

  • It does not diagnose a medical or neurological condition.
  • It does not predict an individual astronaut's cognitive performance.
  • It does not establish causation from CO₂ concentration alone.
  • The displayed values are demonstration values rather than clinical measurements.
  • Real spaceflight research requires controlled experiments, validated measurements, and multiple environmental variables.
PROJECT

About Space NeuroHealth

MISSION

Connecting environmental monitoring with human performance

Space NeuroHealth is a research prototype designed to explore how environmental conditions encountered during spaceflight can be visualized alongside measures of human cognitive performance.

The interface combines an interactive dashboard, scenario controls, charts, research context, and scientific limitations into a single educational experience.
