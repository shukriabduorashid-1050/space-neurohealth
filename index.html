⚠️ Data Integrity & Limitations
  • Uploaded CSV files are processed locally in the browser.
  • A CSV file is not automatically considered NASA data.
  • The prototype checks formatting and numeric values but does not independently verify the scientific origin of a dataset.
  • Correlation or temporal association does not by itself establish causation.

🌌

Space Environment

Spaceflight exposes crew members to a combination of environmental conditions that can influence human performance.

The dashboard is designed to provide a framework for exploring these relationships using validated mission datasets.

🧠

Human Performance

Reaction time and vigilance can be represented using standardized behavioral measurements such as the Psychomotor Vigilance Task.

The integrated PVT tester demonstrates how an interactive performance measurement could fit into the research interface.

📐

Statistical Analysis

Mean, minimum, maximum, and time-series trends provide initial descriptive statistics.

A future research version could add formal statistical tests and validated predictive models.

RESEARCH GAP

From visualization to validated research

The current prototype focuses on data interaction, visualization, and research communication. A competition-ready scientific study should replace demonstration values with documented datasets, define inclusion criteria, establish reproducible preprocessing, compare analytical approaches, and report uncertainty and limitations transparently.

🔎 Future Validation
  • Identify the official NASA or mission dataset.
  • Document the source, variables, units, and collection period.
  • Define data-cleaning and missing-value rules before analysis.
  • Compare proposed methods against appropriate baseline methods.
  • Report statistical uncertainty and limitations.
PROJECT INFORMATION

About Space NeuroHealth

🛰️

Research Prototype

Space NeuroHealth is an interactive research prototype designed to explore how environmental measurements and human cognitive-performance measurements could be brought together in a transparent analytical interface.

The current interface intentionally separates demonstration data from validated research data. This allows the technical system to be developed without presenting simulated values as scientific findings.

🧪

Reproducible

Data processing rules should be documented so another researcher can reproduce the analysis.

📊

Interactive

Users can explore environmental conditions, charts, statistics, and performance testing.

🔐

Local Processing

Uploaded CSV files are processed directly in the browser by this prototype.

🔬

Scientific Caution

Simulated relationships are clearly separated from validated scientific evidence.

FUTURE DEVELOPMENT
